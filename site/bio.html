Wutao Liu刘武韬

Instagram · @alexaander_liu ↗

A bit of an oddball, always a rookie. Drawn to the unknown, eager for new experiences, and curious about all the possibilities life holds.

He loves places where light and shadow meet, and photographs what makes him pause—a passing stranger, a quiet corner, a place he might never return to.

Still learning to see the world. Still curious about what lies elsewhere.

一个有点古怪的人，始终是新手的人。向往未知，期待新鲜的经历，也好奇生活还有多少种可能。

他爱着那些光影交错的地方，并拍下那些让他驻足的事物：擦肩而过的陌生人，安静的街角，或许再也不会重返的地方。

还在学着观察世界，也还在好奇着别处。
